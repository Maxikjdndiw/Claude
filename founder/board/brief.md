# Board brief: Schleswig Bike Share (working name)

This brief stands on its own. Everything below is what the founder said; nothing has been added or checked.

## The idea (from founder/idea.md)

- What it is: An app-based bike-sharing service in Schleswig (Schleswig-Holstein, Germany), starting as a 10-bike pilot.
- Who it is for: Young people in Schleswig, with a discounted plan for pupils.
- What it sells, at what price: Bike rides booked through an app: 1.50 € per ride, or a 12 € monthly subscription (5 € a month for pupils).
- Where and how: Schleswig, in person. Customers find and unlock the bikes through the app; the bikes stay out in the city.
- Budget and constraints: A 10-bike pilot. The city is expected to cover part of the running costs (amount and terms not yet known). Extra revenue is planned from advertising on the bikes (advertisers and rates not yet known). Startup budget, bike and app costs, and who maintains the bikes have not been given.

## The founder's own words

"App bike sharing in Schleswig for young people, 10 bike pilot, 1.50 € per ride or 12 € a month (5 € for pupils), the city covers part of the running costs, and you earn from ads on the bikes."

## Revenue streams named by the founder

1. Pay per ride: 1.50 €.
2. Monthly subscription: 12 € (pupils: 5 €).
3. Advertising on the bikes.
4. A city contribution to running costs.

## Not known yet

Startup budget, cost per bike, app build or licence cost, maintenance and rebalancing, insurance, theft and damage, how much the city pays and on what terms, ad rates and buyers, the number of target customers, existing competitors or substitutes in Schleswig. Do not assume figures for these; name them as open questions.
