Schleswig Bike Share (working name): a new app-based bike-sharing pilot in Schleswig with 10 bikes.
Find a bike in the app, unlock it with your phone, ride, and leave it again when you're done.
1.50 € per ride, or 12 € a month for unlimited rides. Pupils pay 5 € a month.
Pilot phase: 10 bikes in town. You pay in the app.
